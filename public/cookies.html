Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Type Perfectly uses cookies, local storage, analytics, and similar browser technologies.

Our current use

Type Perfectly uses browser local storage to remember typing progress, recent sessions, personal best scores, and selected settings.

Type Perfectly also uses Google Analytics to understand general website traffic and usage. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar browser technologies.

When you create or use an account, Supabase authentication may use browser storage to keep you signed in and securely manage your session.

Local storage

Local storage is a small browser memory area. Type Perfectly may store your personal best, recent sessions, selected options, and related practice information on your device.

This allows the website to remember your progress without requiring you to create an account. Clearing browser data may remove locally saved progress.

Analytics cookies

Google Analytics may collect limited information such as device type, browser type, visited pages, approximate location, referral source, and website usage.

We use this information to understand and improve the website. We do not intentionally use advertising cookies at this time.

Account and authentication storage

If you create an account, Supabase may use browser storage or essential authentication technologies to keep you signed in, protect your session, and provide account features.

How to control browser storage

You can clear cookies and local storage through your browser settings. Doing so may sign you out and remove saved Type Perfectly progress and preferences from that device.

Future changes

We may update this Cookie Policy if Type Perfectly adds new analytics, advertising, account, or storage technologies.

Contact

Questions about cookies or browser storage may be sent to contact@typeperfectly.com .
